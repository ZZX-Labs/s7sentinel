# Code of Conduct

S7Sentinel contributors are expected to collaborate professionally, focus criticism on technical work rather than people, respect privacy and confidentiality, and avoid harassment or discriminatory conduct.

Security research must be handled with particular care. Do not pressure contributors to disclose sensitive targets, customer environments, unpublished vulnerabilities, or identifying incident data. Do not use project spaces to coordinate unauthorized access or harmful activity.

Maintainers may edit or remove disruptive content, close abusive interactions, and restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and the project.

Questions about moderation should be directed privately to the repository maintainers.
